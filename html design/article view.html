Compliance Global Standards

Electrical Approval Workflow

A standardized procedure for verifying and certifying electrical installations in mission-critical enterprise environments. This guide ensures all compliance nodes are satisfied before project activation.

Certification Safety Workflow

Approval Steps

1

Initial Documentation Review

Collect all single-line diagrams, load schedules, and panel board layouts. Ensure all files are in high-resolution PDF format and signed by the Lead Engineer.

attachment Download Checklist Template
2

Site Assessment Verification

Perform a physical walkthrough of the installation site. Verify that grounding systems meet the specific impedance requirements (typically < 5 ohms) for the equipment sensitive loads.

3

Final Safety Testing

Execute insulation resistance tests and continuity tests across all major branch circuits. Documentation of these values must be uploaded to the 'Compliance Report' section of the article.

4

Digital Certificate Issuance

Once all prior steps are verified by the CRM automated agent, trigger the 'Generate Certificate' button. This will email a cryptographicly signed PDF to all project stakeholders.
